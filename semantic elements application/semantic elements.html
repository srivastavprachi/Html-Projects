About GeeksForGeeks

GeeksforGeeks (GFG) is a leading online platform dedicated to computer science and programming education. It was founded in 2009 by Sandeep Jain, an alumnus of IIT Roorkee.

What GFG Offers:

  • In-depth tutorials on Data Structures, Algorithms, DBMS, OS, and more.
  • Practice problems to improve coding and problem-solving skills.
  • Expert-designed courses for students and professionals.
  • Interview preparation resources with real-world company questions.
  • Regular coding contests and challenges.
  • Technical blogs and articles by the developer community.

Mission:

To make quality computer science education accessible and effective for all learners across the globe.

Community:

GeeksforGeeks has built a strong and growing global community of students, educators, and professionals who learn, code, and share together.
